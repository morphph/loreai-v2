### Karpathy's Prompt for Clearer Model Explanations

A better explanation may start with tighter writing rules. **Karpathy** suggests asking a model to explain a topic in ASD-STE100, a controlled language that restricts wording and sentence structure. Try it on a dense answer, then check whether the simpler prose preserves the technical meaning. (39,386 likes | 4,306 RTs) [Read more →](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479)

**ServiceNow’s AutoSynthData generates agent training data.** Enterprise agents need examples of the tasks they will actually face; **ServiceNow AI** presents a method for creating that data. Teams with thin or uneven task datasets should examine the method before collecting more examples by hand. [Read more →](https://huggingface.co/blog/ServiceNow-AI/autosynthdata)

**Coding agent maintenance gets a schedule.** A proposed set of **Claude Code** commands turns checks for duplicate code, tangled structure, and stale agent rules into recurring work. An answer key cannot grade work on an unsolved problem. **Meta** says mathematicians are working with **Muse Spark** on open math questions after its models reached gold-medal-level performance across five competitions in mathematics, physics, and chemistry. The result to watch is what the mathematicians can verify and build on, rather than a competition score. (502 likes | 70 RTs) [Read more →](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2106099776035152231)

## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Ground truth in open-ended evaluation** means deciding what counts as a correct result when no answer key exists. On an unsolved math problem, a plausible proof from **Muse Spark** is a proposal, not a scored answer. Mathematicians must check each step, look for gaps or counterexamples, and judge whether any valid contribution advances the problem. That human verification is the measure that matters here.
