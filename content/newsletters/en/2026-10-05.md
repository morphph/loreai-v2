### Put Hard Budget Caps on Agent Workflows

An agent that keeps working can keep spending. **Hard budget caps** give a run a firm stopping point, and Simon Willison argues they should become a default across systems that consume resources autonomously. Review your workflows for calls that can repeat, fan out, or wait indefinitely; each needs a limit someone chose deliberately. [Read more →](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/3/default-hard-budget-caps/)

## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Agent execution traces** record an agent's tool calls and decisions as it works. They help a team reconstruct a disputed action: what the agent attempted, what each tool returned, and what happened next. Paired with spending records, a trace can also show where a budget cap should have stopped a run. The useful trace is one a human can inspect when the outcome is contested.
