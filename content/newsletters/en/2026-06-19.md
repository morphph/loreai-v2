## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Low-Rank Adaptation Variants (DoRA, PiSSA, rsLoRA)**: LoRA fine-tunes large models cheaply by injecting small trainable matrices into frozen layers — but it's no longer the only game in town. **DoRA** (Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation) splits weight updates into magnitude and direction components, often outperforming vanilla LoRA on tasks that need precise directional adjustments. **PiSSA** (Principal Singular Value Adaptation) initializes the low-rank matrices using the principal components of the original weights rather than random values, giving training a head start. **rsLoRA** scales the learning rate by rank, fixing a subtle issue where higher-rank adaptations learn too slowly. The practical upshot: PEFT method selection is now a real engineering decision — defaulting to vanilla LoRA without benchmarking alternatives means you might be leaving performance on the table.
