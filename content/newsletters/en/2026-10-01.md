## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Functional watermarking** means embedding a detectable signal in a generated design while preserving what that design is meant to do. With proteins, the challenge is especially concrete: a watermark that changes biological function defeats the point. **SynthID Bio** raises the question of how to make a generated protein traceable without sacrificing its intended behavior. A convincing evaluation has to check both detectability and function.

## 🎯 PICK OF THE DAY

**Protein watermarks could make provenance part of the design process.** If generated proteins can carry traceable marks without losing their function, provenance could become something designers build in from the start rather than check after deployment. **SynthID Bio** is a proof of concept, so it does not settle how broadly the method will work. Still, it tackles the right constraint: in biology, a detectable signature is worthless if adding it breaks the molecule. That makes this a more consequential idea than another claim that a model can generate candidates faster. [Read more →](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-synthid-bio/)
