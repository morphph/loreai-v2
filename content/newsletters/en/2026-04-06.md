## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Neural Audio Codecs**: OpenAI's next-gen audio models and OmniVoice both rely on a shared breakthrough: neural audio codecs. Traditional audio compression (MP3, AAC) uses hand-designed algorithms to shrink files. Neural audio codecs instead train a model to compress speech into discrete tokens — small integers that a language model can reason over, generate, and manipulate just like text tokens. This is the bridge that lets a single transformer handle both language and speech in one architecture. It's why voice AI quality is leaping so fast: once audio becomes tokens, every advance in language modeling — longer context, better reasoning, faster inference — automatically improves voice too.
