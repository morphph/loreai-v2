## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Model Rollback and Staged Redeployment**: Fable 5's pull-and-redeploy cycle this week illustrates a practice that's becoming standard at frontier labs: staged rollouts with circuit breakers. Instead of one-shot launches, models now go through graduated deployment — limited access first, monitoring for safety and performance regressions, then wider release. When something flags, the model gets pulled and redeployed after fixes, not patched in place. For builders, this means designing around availability gaps: hardcode fallback models, use model routing with automatic failover, and never assume a specific model will be available 100% of the time. The industry is trading launch-day availability for long-term reliability — plan accordingly.
