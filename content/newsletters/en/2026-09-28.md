## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Leaderboard score versus task fit**: A leaderboard such as Text Arena compares model outputs through a shared evaluation process and turns those comparisons into a ranking. It can reveal a meaningful change in how a model performs there. It cannot tell you how that model handles your codebase, images, budget, or response-time target. Use the ranking to decide what to test next, then run the tasks you actually need done.
