## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Text watermarking and detection reliability:** A text watermark aims to leave a detectable pattern in generated writing. A detector may find evidence consistent with that pattern, but editing, translation, short passages, and differences between generators can weaken the signal. A positive or negative result alone cannot prove who authored a text. That distinction matters as provenance signals enter compliance workflows.

## 🎯 PICK OF THE DAY

**OpenAI’s text-provenance expansion exposes a crucial gap.** A watermark could become a compliance signal before it becomes a reliable test of authorship. Those are different jobs: one may help show that a provider took a required step, while the other asks whether a particular person or model wrote particular words. OpenAI’s own acknowledgment of current limits is the reason to take this seriously, not a reason to dismiss it. If you build verification into a product, define the claim your signal supports before a user treats a detection result as proof.
