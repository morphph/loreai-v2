## 🎓 MODEL LITERACY

**Model Weights vs. API Access**: The Fable ban was possible because API access can be revoked with a single government directive — one phone call, and millions of users lose access overnight. Open-weight models like Llama or Mistral work differently: once you've downloaded the weights, no one can "turn them off." The model runs on your hardware, under your control. This distinction — who holds the kill switch — is now the central question in AI infrastructure decisions. When you build on an API, you're renting capability that can be revoked. When you run open weights, you own the capability but bear the cost and complexity. After this week, every engineering team should know exactly where their AI stack falls on that spectrum.
