🎓 MODEL LITERACY

Pareto Frontier in Model Evaluation: Today's open-model roundup ranks Laguna S2.1, Inkling, and Kimi K3 on the Pareto frontier — the curve where no model dominates another on every metric at once. A model is "on the frontier" if improving one dimension (say, coding ability) would require sacrificing another (say, cost or latency). Models below the frontier are strictly worse than at least one frontier model on all measured axes. Understanding this concept explains why "best model" is always the wrong question — the real question is which tradeoffs you're willing to accept for your use case.
