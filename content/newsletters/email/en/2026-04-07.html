MODEL LITERACY

Model Quantization (NVFP4): When NVIDIA quantized Gemma 4 31B to NVFP4 format, they shrank the model's weights by 4x — turning a model that needed a multi-GPU server into one that runs on a single consumer GPU. Quantization works by reducing the numerical precision of each weight (from 16-bit or 32-bit floating point down to 4-bit), trading tiny amounts of accuracy for massive memory savings. The reason official vendor quantizations like NVFP4 matter more than community ones: NVIDIA calibrates the compression against known benchmarks and optimizes for their hardware's specific number formats, resulting in less quality loss than a generic quantization recipe. If a model's weights fit in your GPU's VRAM, you can run it locally — and understanding quantization formats is how you know which models will actually fit.
