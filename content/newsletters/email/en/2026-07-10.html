MODEL LITERACY

Model Tiering vs. Model Versioning: GPT-5.6 ships as Luna, Terra, and Sol — three capability tiers under one version number. The industry is shifting from "upgrade to the next number" to "pick the right tier for your task." Luna handles simple classification and summarization at a fraction of Sol's cost, while Sol tackles frontier reasoning and complex agentic workflows. Understanding price-performance frontiers across tiers now matters more than chasing the biggest model. The developer who routes a summarization job to Sol is overpaying; the one who sends a multi-step research task to Luna is getting garbage. Tier selection is the new model selection.
