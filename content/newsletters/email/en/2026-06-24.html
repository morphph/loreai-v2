2. Simon Willison Reframes Prompt Injection as Role Confusion

Stop calling it "injection." Simon Willison argues the real problem is role confusion — when models lose track of which inputs are instructions and which are data. "Injection" implies the fix is input sanitization (it isn't); "role confusion" points you toward architectural separation of instruction and data channels. If you're building agents that process untrusted input, this mental model will change how you design your prompt boundaries. →

🎓 MODEL LITERACY

Content Provenance (C2PA): Claude Tag launches today on top of the C2PA standard — the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity. C2PA works by attaching cryptographic signatures to content at creation, then chaining those signatures through every subsequent edit, crop, or re-export. Each link in the chain records who modified what and when, creating a tamper-evident trail that anyone downstream can verify. Unlike AI detection tools that guess whether something is synthetic, C2PA doesn't care about detection — it proves provenance through cryptography. Think of it as HTTPS for content: not "is this real?" but "can this content prove where it came from?"

🎯 PICK OF THE DAY

Reframing prompt injection as "role confusion" isn't just semantics — it's a strategic shift. Simon Willison's piece does something rare: it takes a problem the entire industry has been banging its head against for three years and gives it a better name that actually changes how you think about solutions. "Prompt injection" implies the fix is input sanitization — filter the bad stuff out before it reaches the model. But as anyone who's tried knows, that's a losing game. "Role confusion" reframes the problem architecturally: the model loses track of what's an instruction and what's data. That points you toward structural separation of instruction and data channels, not cleverer input filters. For anyone building autonomous agents that process untrusted input (which is eventually all of them), this mental model is load-bearing. Read it, internalize it, and audit your agent prompts for role-boundary clarity. →
