# How Do Consulting Firms Join the Claude Partner Network?

Consulting firms join the **[Claude Partner Network](/blog/anthropic-claude-partner-network-100-million)** by applying through Anthropic's official partner program. Anthropic evaluates firms based on their technical expertise with Claude, track record of enterprise AI deployments, and ability to help customers implement Claude responsibly across their organizations.

## Context

Anthropic launched the Claude Partner Network to build an ecosystem of consulting and services firms that can help enterprises adopt Claude effectively. The program recognizes that many organizations — particularly large enterprises — rely on consulting partners to evaluate, integrate, and scale AI tools rather than building in-house expertise from scratch.

The partner network includes major global consulting firms as well as specialized AI consultancies. Partners get access to technical resources, early product previews, co-marketing opportunities, and direct lines to Anthropic's engineering and go-to-market teams. For Anthropic, the network extends their reach into enterprise accounts where consulting firms already have established relationships.

This program reflects a broader industry pattern: as AI models become more capable, the bottleneck shifts from model quality to deployment expertise. Firms that understand [AI safety](/glossary/ai-safety) principles and responsible deployment practices are particularly valued, given Anthropic's emphasis on safe AI development. For more on Anthropic's strategic direction, see the [Anthropic topic hub](/topics/anthropic).

## Practical Steps

1. **Check eligibility**: Anthropic looks for firms with demonstrated experience in AI/ML consulting, enterprise software integration, or digital transformation
2. **Apply through Anthropic's website**: Visit Anthropic's partner page and submit an application detailing your firm's capabilities, client base, and relevant case studies
3. **Demonstrate Claude expertise**: Firms with existing Claude API usage, completed client projects using Claude, or certified technical staff have stronger applications
4. **Align on responsible AI practices**: Anthropic prioritizes partners who share their commitment to safe and responsible AI deployment — be prepared to discuss your approach to AI governance and risk management
5. **Engage with onboarding**: Accepted partners go through a technical enablement process, gaining access to partner-specific resources, training materials, and support channels
