# What Is the Claude Partner Network?

The **[Claude Partner Network](/blog/anthropic-claude-partner-network-100-million)** is Anthropic's formal ecosystem of consulting firms, system integrators, and technology partners that help enterprises adopt and deploy [Claude](/glossary/chatgpt)-family models at scale. It connects organizations that want to build with Claude to vetted partners who have deep experience implementing AI solutions — covering everything from initial strategy through production deployment and ongoing optimization.

## Context

Anthropic built the Claude Partner Network to address a gap between raw model capability and enterprise readiness. Large organizations rarely adopt AI tools by simply signing up for an API key. They need architecture guidance, security reviews, compliance frameworks, change management, and custom integration work. The partner network fills that gap.

The network includes several categories of partners:

- **Consulting and strategy firms** — major consultancies that advise enterprises on AI transformation and help identify high-value Claude use cases
- **System integrators** — technical partners that build custom Claude-powered applications, handle data pipelines, and integrate with existing enterprise infrastructure
- **Technology partners** — cloud providers and platform companies (notably AWS via Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud via Vertex AI) that offer Claude through their managed AI services
- **ISVs (Independent Software Vendors)** — software companies that embed Claude's capabilities into their own products

This partner model mirrors what other major AI companies have done — OpenAI has a similar program — but Anthropic's network reflects its emphasis on [AI safety](/glossary/ai-safety) and responsible deployment. Partners are expected to follow Anthropic's usage policies and help customers implement appropriate safeguards.

## Practical Steps

1. **If you're an enterprise evaluating Claude**: Browse Anthropic's partner directory to find a partner with experience in your industry and use case
2. **If you're a consulting or tech firm**: Apply through Anthropic's partner program page — typical requirements include demonstrated AI implementation experience and alignment with Anthropic's responsible use guidelines
3. **If you're already building with Claude**: Consider whether a partner can accelerate your deployment, especially for compliance-sensitive industries like healthcare, finance, or government
4. **For cloud-native deployments**: Evaluate whether accessing Claude through a technology partner like AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI simplifies your infrastructure and procurement
